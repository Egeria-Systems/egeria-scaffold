# Builder core boundary

Read the repository [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md), the canonical [package ownership](../../docs/architecture/package-ownership.md), and the [review and contribution protocol](../../docs/governance/review-and-contribution.md) before editing this surface.

`packages/builder-core` is the private owner of builder internals, project/state schemas, and the private certification registry contract. Its current executable boundary includes checked JSON Schema artifacts, the exact seven-capability catalog, exact `standards@0.3.0`/recipe `0.9.0` upgrade-source snapshots, current `portfolio` and `site` recipe `0.10.0` with readable retained `0.1.0` through `0.9.0` provenance, deterministic resolution, installed-manifest projection, strict `.egeria` codecs including `capability-upgrade` and `profile-transition` receipts, subject-bound descriptor admission and closure decisions, pure hybrid-ownership fingerprint materialization, fixed-root read-only repository access, deterministic capability and surface evidence, content-safe read-only diagnostics and structural differences, Git preflight, deterministic addition, removal, exact supported-upgrade planning, and exact `portfolio@0.10.0` to `site@0.10.0` profile-transition planning, exact-diff inspection, exact supported-upgrade and profile-transition execution, the state-last `booking-calendly@0.1.0` addition/removal transactions, exact `standards@0.3.0` to `standards@0.4.0` transaction, and exact `portfolio@0.10.0` to `site@0.10.0` transaction, deterministic in-memory rendering from explicit allowlisted templates with text and binary entries, and state-last generation into a previously absent destination. Generation uses exact verified public package versions: standards `0.1.0` and observability `0.3.0`, with an injected verifier boundary.

- Keep runtime Zod schemas canonical and generated Draft 2020-12 artifacts byte-checked against them. Do not hand-edit artifacts.
- Keep certification records keyed by the exact executable capability identifier in [`certifications/capabilities.json`](../../certifications/capabilities.json). Bind each subject to its descriptor version and canonical descriptor/evidence-contract digest. Bind each passed evidence entry and accepted receipt to the capability, subject, evidence-producing ancestor revision, and explicit passed and reviewed outcome. Keep plans and pre-acceptance artifacts private; track a registry-cited final receipt only after explicit acceptance, content-safe review, and an exact ignore exception. Run `pnpm run check:private-capability-certification` before a certification workflow updates the registry; it rejects missing, incomplete, unresolved, negatively reviewed, or non-ancestor artifacts. Clean-checkout CI validates the tracked registry contract, descriptor admission, and closure without opening private ignored artifacts. Registry presence, admission, local evidence, or `backfill-pending` never implies certification.
- Keep descriptor admission distinct from closure. Exact `standards@0.4.0` is certified from its accepted local `fresh-scaffold` receipt at evidence revision `416e2c2441978ac86f3a17dee96a694141033e20`; `booking-calendly@0.1.0`, `observability@0.3.0`, and `deployment-cloudflare@0.3.0` remain certified, and three unchanged subjects retain frozen `backfill-pending` records. The prior standards and observability receipts remain evidence only for their exact earlier subjects. Admission and `legacy-backfill-exempt` closure pass, while `all-certified` closure rejects only the three frozen backfills.
- Keep the executable catalog limited to `standards`, `content-files`, `section-composition`, `deployment-cloudflare`, `observability`, `site-routing`, and `booking-calendly`; keep executable profiles limited to `portfolio` and `site`.
- Keep generation package versions builder-owned, immutable, exact, and separately verified. Project-generation requests must not accept caller package versions.
- Keep reads fixed to explicitly declared control, probe, valid-state surface, and exact profile-transition action paths. The reader does not enumerate or write, follows no requested-path symlink, caps both text and byte reads at 1 MiB, and emits no source content through evidence. Exact byte reads exist only to compare transition action subjects, including binary visual baselines; they do not create a general scanner.
- Keep inference evidence separate from policy. Read-only diagnostics compose existing codecs, resolution, and inference; they neither authorize nor perform a repository change.
- Keep `planCapabilityAddition` limited to deterministic eligibility and action planning for `booking-calendly@0.1.0`. Require a clean attached linked worktree, detect tracked changes, all non-ignored untracked files, and the `assume-unchanged` and `skip-worktree` index flags. Require the installed state to match the exact canonical managed-surface inventory and every installed application-owned surface to remain a readable regular file, without comparing application-owned content fingerprints. Refuse Git operations/conflicts, current managed drift, missing surfaces, ejections, present collisions, absent but Git-ignored create targets, unstable identity, and unexpected Git or reader failures with their exact content-safe boundary codes. Redact settings and repository metadata. Planning performs no transform, migration, state update, or provider action and does not create a branch or worktree.
- Keep `applyCapabilityAddition` limited to consuming the exact plan fingerprint for `booking-calendly@0.1.0` in that same eligible linked worktree. Recompute and revalidate the plan, write only its seven actions, run ordinary verification in a VCS-metadata-free isolated copy, require the exact bounded pending-add inference and expected transformed bytes, append the successful migration record, persist state last, require final state/inference, accept only the exact expected dirty path set, and make an exact changed-byte reread the final awaited check. Persisted receipts name only checks completed before their write; the returned success envelope adds migration-record and post-state checks. Preserve prior control state through transformation, verification, and re-inference failures. Never auto-reset, clean, rollback, commit, create a branch/worktree, or contact a provider; stable recovery output directs inspection of the isolated worktree.
- Keep `planCapabilityUpgrade` limited to the exact repository-only `standards@0.3.0` to `standards@0.4.0` capability edge. Infer the source version; bind the accepted recipe/catalog endpoint snapshots and subject digests; require exact desired, installed, inferred, project, state, migration, inventory, and no-ejection agreement; derive only the six standards-owned target actions; and bind exact control bytes, target fingerprints, and private Git identity into the redacted plan fingerprint. Refuse already-current, missing-edge, unsupported, ambiguous-version, incompatible-state, drift, collision, dirty, unstable, detached, or non-isolated cases without mutation. Planning performs no transformation, verification, migration, persistence, upgrade execution, generic lifecycle extraction, provider action, branch creation, or worktree creation.
- Keep `planProfileTransition` limited to the exact read-only `portfolio@0.10.0` to `site@0.10.0` edge. Infer the source profile from exact project, state, migration, desired, installed, inferred, inventory, ejection, and rendered-source agreement; preserve exact optional `booking-calendly@0.1.0` selection and settings; derive only the two creates and five replacements owned by `site-routing`, `content-files`, and `standards`; and bind all three raw and parsed control files, endpoint recipes, catalogs, manifests, surfaces, action bytes, the public plan, and private Git identity into the plan fingerprint. Refuse already-current, unsupported-source, missing-edge, unsupported-target, ambiguous-inference, incompatible-state, drift, ejection, collision, unsafe-Git, or unstable-identity cases without mutation. Planning performs no transformation, verification, migration, persistence, generic lifecycle extraction, provider action, branch creation, or worktree creation.
- Keep `applyCapabilityUpgrade` and its private `createFileSystemCapabilityUpgradeWriter` operation-specific and limited to that same single edge. - Keep `prepareMigrationRecord`, `persistMigrationRecord`, and `persistInstalledState` private and limited to the canonical control-persistence mechanics evidenced across the four accepted executors. - Do not expose builder-core as a public package or add generic `PlatformService` or `ApplicationDatabase` ports.
- Follow the currently approved increment and stop at its review gate before expanding this boundary.
